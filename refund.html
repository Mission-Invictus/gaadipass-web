Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Cancellation

You can cancel an order any time before it is dispatched for a full refund. Once dispatched, the order cannot be cancelled.

Damaged or wrong item

If your stickers arrive damaged, defective or wrong, tell us within 7 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a free replacement or give a full refund.

Service not working

If the QR code or calling does not work for your tag within 7 days of delivery and we cannot fix it, we will refund the order in full.

Non-returnable

Stickers that have already been applied to a vehicle cannot be returned, except in the cases above.

Refund timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days to the original payment method.

How to request

Email founder@missioninvictus.com with your order details.
