Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how Mission Invictus ("we") handles personal data for GaadiPass.

What we collect

  • Order details: name, delivery address, phone number, email, vehicle number (optional).
  • Activation: the mobile number linked to your tag and any backup contact you add.
  • Call and alert logs: time, duration and tag of each call/alert. Caller numbers are stored only to prevent abuse.
  • Payment: processed by our payment partner. We do not store card details or UPI PINs.

How we use it

To deliver orders, connect masked calls and alerts, prevent abuse, provide support and meet legal obligations. We never show your number to callers and we never sell your data.

Sharing

Only with service providers needed to run GaadiPass (telephony, payments, delivery, hosting) under confidentiality, or when required by law.

Retention

Order data is kept as long as accounting and tax law requires. Call logs are kept for up to 12 months. You can ask us to delete your tag and data at any time.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data by writing to founder@missioninvictus.com.
