## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: individual car owners in Bengaluru, mostly living in apartment societies (Sarjapur Road, HSR, Whitefield, Bellandur, Electronic City and similar), buying GaadiPass for their own car. They reach the site from a Meta (Click-to-WhatsApp / link) ad or a society WhatsApp group, almost always on a phone, often mid-scroll with little patience. Their job: stop the daily friction of "someone blocked my car / I blocked someone and nobody can reach me" without putting their phone number on the windshield.

Secondary (mentioned, not the lead): society / RWA admins buying in bulk for residents.

## Product Purpose

GaadiPass is a windshield sticker with a unique QR code plus a printed phone number and tag code. Anyone who needs the owner (blocked car, lights left on, towing, accident) can reach them through a masked call or alert; neither side sees the other's number. Success = a visitor understands it in seconds, trusts it, and completes an order (₹399 for 2 stickers, paid by UPI with a screenshot upload).

## Positioning

- Works even without a smartphone: dial the number on the sticker and enter the tag code (most competitors are QR-scan only).
- Number stays private (masked call bridge).
- Family call order (owner → backup contact → alert).
- Made for apartment-society parking.
- No monthly fee; one-time price.

## Product Principles

1. Understood in 5 seconds on a phone: problem → sticker → phone rings, shown visually before explained.
2. Trust through honesty: clear price, policies, real business details; no invented proof.
3. Ordering must feel as easy as a UPI payment: few fields, big tap targets, the right app opens with the amount filled.
4. Privacy is the product: always show that numbers stay hidden.
5. Lightweight: static, fast, works on cheap Android and iPhone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Mobile-first; large readable text and tap targets (≥44px); good contrast in sunlight; works without JS for reading pages; forms with clear labels and error messages; users with limited English benefit from visuals and short sentences.
